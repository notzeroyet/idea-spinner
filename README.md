# Idea Spinner

Turn a niche into shirt sayings. Fill in what they do, who they are or what they love, and the Idea Spinner
gives you popular phrases to pick from, questions that pull out words nobody else has, and starting titles and
tags for your Etsy listing.

**Use it online, nothing to download:** https://notzeroyet.com/idea-spinner/

**Video tutorial:** [Watch the Idea Spinner walkthrough on YouTube](https://youtu.be/d5xb9wKOtu0)

## Open your own copy

The whole tool is one file, `index.html`. There's nothing to install and no account to make.

1. On this page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip it. On a Mac, double click the ZIP file. On Windows, right click it and choose **Extract All**.
3. Open the unzipped folder and double click `index.html`. It opens in your web browser.

It works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on Mac, Windows, Linux and Chromebook. On a Chromebook, open
the file from the Files app. On a phone, use the website instead, since phones don't open files from a ZIP
very well.

The first time you open it you need an internet connection so the fonts can load. After that the tool runs
in your browser on your own computer.

## How to use it

1. **Who is the Design For?** Fill in one, two or all three fields. Have a list from the
   [Niche Library](https://notzeroyet.com/niche-library/)? Paste it in and each idea becomes a button.
2. **Popular Winning Phrases.** Select the phrases you like. Press **Spin for 12 More** for new ones, or pick a
   mood like Funny or Holiday. You can also add your own phrase.
3. **Your Own Words.** Answer a question about the people you're designing for, and your words turn into POD
   phrases for your niche.
4. **Your Listing Title and Tags.** Starting words for your listing. These listing titles aren't researched, so
   press Etsy or Amazon to see what's already out there, then make adjustments as you see fit.
5. **Your List.** Press **Copy for TypePress** and paste it into [TypePress](https://notzeroyet.com/typepress/)
   to make the designs, or **Download My List** to keep everything.

Check each phrase for trademarks before you list it, in the
[US trademark search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/). Every Etsy listing costs $0.20, so it's up to you to do the
math on how many designs you'd like.

## Your data

Your list stays in your own browser, on your own computer. There's no account, and the downloaded copy sends
nothing anywhere. (The website version counts visits with Google Analytics; the downloaded copy doesn't.)
